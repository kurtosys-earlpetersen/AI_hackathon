Schema Source

Portfolio-level metric

The SSGA nexus_sustainability dataset is sourced from VWS_ESG_PORTFOLIO_CHARACTERISTICS and returns portfolio-level sustainability metrics, including carbon footprint, total emissions, WACI, climate VaR, implied temperature rise, brown revenues, and R-Factor.

Water Constraint

Future illustrative metric

Water stress is not currently present in the SSGA nexus_sustainability schema. It is intentionally excluded from primary outputs here and should only be shown as a clearly labelled future extension.

Holdings Constraint

Separate app boundary

Holdings data lives in nexus_holdings_view / nexus_holdings_export using FUND_ID. Sustainability data uses CRS_FUND_ALIAS. There is no schema-supported endpoint combining holdings weights with per-security carbon footprint values.

Illustrative prototype for hackathon purposes only. The displayed values use schema-aligned mock data from SSGA sustainability exports plus the supplied SSIM sample methodology categories, and are not investment advice, a solicitation, a production financed-emissions methodology, or a guarantee of actual avoided emissions. Sector sliders, donut charts, and product involvement charts are illustrative visualizations of sample categories, not evidence of a production per-holding ESG endpoint. All water-related functionality is excluded from primary outputs because water stress is not currently available in the SSGA sustainability schema.
